Nine days up the Karakoram Highway to Hunza and Skardu

Headlines simply appear (the word-by-word reveal is used only on the homepage brand statement)

We drive up from Lahore on the motorway and the Karakoram Highway. Then we spend three nights in Hunza under Rakaposhi, and cross to Skardu for its lakes and the high Deosai plains.

This is a road trip at heart. The drive between stops is part of the trip: the Indus gorge, the place where three mountain ranges meet, the turquoise of Attabad. Your driver and guide set the pace, plan around the weather and know where to stop for chai.

Who this trip is for
  • +First-time visitors who want Hunza and Skardu in one trip
  • +Families with children [X]+ and parents who like a planned pace

Who it may not suit
  • –Travellers who dislike long road journeys. Days 2 and 9 are [10+] hours

  • –Serious heart or breathing conditions at altitude. Check with your doctor first
  • –Travellers who need luxury hotels every night; Chilas is simple
