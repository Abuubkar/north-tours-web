01 Principles
Dark for imagery
Hero, map, photography and destination sections sit on Ink 900. Photos supply the colour.
Light for reading
Help, policies, Legal, the Trip Planner form, “Good to know” and Tour Detail practical sections sit on Mist 50.
Gold means action
Primary buttons, ratings, active nav, map markers and key highlights. Never decoration, never large areas.
One typeface
Geist. Large and tight for headlines, small and clean for everything else.
Hairlines, not boxes
1px lines divide sections and grid cells. No shadows.
Headlines carry the section
No small labels repeating the headline. Plain, specific, varied in length, mostly without a full stop.
Essentials never wait
Price, dates, seats and WhatsApp are visible on first paint.

07 Lines & placeholders
Section labels

Removed. The headline introduces the section. A small label is used only when the section has no headline (e.g. Credentials, Quick links, footer Contact).

Headlines

Plain and specific, varied in length, mostly without a full stop. At most two short two-beat lines on the whole site (“Your dates, your group”, “Hunza, on your own dates”). Headlines over ~44 characters use the long size: clamp(32px, 3.9cqi, 56px), line-height 1.08, −0.03em, max 3 lines.

PHOTO · Drone shot: Attabad Lake at golden hour

Every placeholder names the exact shot. Real photos and footage only: no AI-generated images of real places or people.

Cards are visible by default; JS adds the offset only to cards below the fold, so nothing hides if the script fails. Hero blur (scroll-linked)Homepage hero video blurs 0 → 16px and darkens to 80% as it scrolls away. Not an entrance animation.Reduced motion: static.

10 Don’ts
✕ No orange accentThe old apricot #D9B44A is retired. Gold #D9B44A on dark, deep gold #7A5A12 on light.
✕ No pill main buttonsButtons are 6px. 999px is for filter chips and small tags only.
✕ No labels repeating headlinesDrop the small label when the headline already says it.
✕ No word-by-word reveals beyond the homepageOnly the homepage brand statement reveals. Everything else simply appears.
✕ No AI-generated images of real places or peopleUse labelled placeholders until real photos and footage arrive.
✕ No gold decorationNo gold backgrounds, borders for show, or filled urgency badges that look like buttons.
✕ No warm cream surfacesLight sections use cool, neutral Mist 50, never beige or cream.
